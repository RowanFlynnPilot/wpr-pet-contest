# Get [Pet] seen: a toolkit for entrants

Thanks for entering. Most votes in this contest come from people who know the
pet, which means they come from you sharing. This page has what you need:
your tools, what to post at each stage, and how to ask without wearing anyone
out.

## The short version

- When you post on Facebook, use the link from your approval email. It shows
  [Pet]'s photo in the preview.
- Make a share image on [Pet]'s page: a story, a square post or a printable
  flyer.
- Share once at each stage of the contest. Every hour is too often.
- Tell people plainly how it works: vote packs from $5, 50% of vote pack sales
  after refunds to Moments Foster Care, and vote totals reset every round.

## Your tools

Your share link is in the email that said [Pet] was approved. Post that one on
Facebook, and the preview will show [Pet]'s photo.

[Pet]'s page has Facebook, Text, Email and Copy link buttons. The Facebook
button uses the same photo link. Copy link copies the contest page's address,
which works well in a text or an email. On Facebook it shows the contest's
picture instead of [Pet]'s.

"Make a share image," also on [Pet]'s page, draws three things:

| Image | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Story | Instagram and Facebook stories |
| Square | A post on your own timeline |
| Flyer | Print it on regular 8.5 by 11 paper. The QR code opens [Pet]'s page |

The image changes with the contest. Before voting it says when voting opens.
During a round it shows [Pet]'s votes and the closing time, so make a fresh one
each round. After a round it says how [Pet] did.

Ask before you hang a flyer at work, at the vet's office or in a shop window,
and take it down when the contest ends.

## When people ask

How do I vote? Tap the link, tap "Vote for [Pet]," pick a pack and pay by card,
Apple Pay or Google Pay. The votes usually show up within seconds of the
payment going through.

How much is it? Packs run from $5 for 5 votes to $250 for 600. Nobody needs the
big ones.

Where does the money go? 50% of vote pack sales, after refunds, goes to
Moments Foster Care. The rest covers payment processing and supports
Wausau Pilot & Review, a nonprofit newsroom.

Is it a donation? No. You're buying votes, so a vote pack is a purchase, and
it isn't tax-deductible.

Do votes carry over? No. Vote totals reset every round. The Open Round decides
which 16 pets make the bracket and their seeds. In the bracket, every matchup
starts at zero.

What's the deadline? Each round closes at a posted time, Central. A vote
counts if its payment goes through by then. A payment that goes through later
is refunded automatically.

What about ties? In the Open Round, the pet entered earlier ranks higher. In
the bracket, the better seed moves on.

What does the champion get? The title, Cutest Pet in Marathon County [year],
and a year of bragging rights.

Something's wrong? Email pets@wausaupilotandreview.com or call 715-301-5539.

## What to share at each stage

One post on your own timeline per stage, plus a text to the people who'd want
to hear. Each stage below has a text, a Facebook post and a story idea. Fill in
the brackets from [Pet]'s page right before you post, so the numbers are true.

### 1. [Pet] is approved, and voting hasn't started

People can look but can't vote yet, so ask them to look.

Text:
> [Pet] is in the Cutest Pet in Marathon County contest! Voting opens [date].
> Here's the entry: [link]

Facebook:
> [Pet] is officially entered in Wausau Pilot & Review's Cutest Pet in Marathon
> County contest. Voting opens [date], and I'll post the link again then. 30%
> of vote pack sales goes to Moments Foster Care. [link]

Story: the Story image (it says when voting opens), with a link sticker.

### 2. The Open Round opens

Start with the people closest to you.

Text:
> Voting's open! If you'd like to vote for [Pet], packs start at $5 and 30%
> goes to Moments Foster Care: [link]. The top 16 pets make the bracket on
> [day].

Facebook:
> Voting is open, and [Pet] is in the Open Round with every other pet in the
> contest. The top 16 make the bracket when it closes [day] at 8 p.m. Vote
> packs start at $5, and 50% of vote pack sales after refunds goes to Moments
> Foster Care. Vote totals reset every round, so this round is about making
> the bracket. [link]

Story: a fresh Story image, which now shows [Pet]'s votes, with a link
sticker.

### 3. Midway through the Open Round

An honest update reaches the next circle: coworkers, old classmates, your
groups.

Facebook:
> Update on [Pet]: No. [rank] of [N] as of [time], [n] votes [above the
> bracket line / from the bracket]. Thank you to everyone who's voted. The Open
> Round closes [day] at 8 p.m. [link]

If a Double Vote Hour is scheduled, add:
> Tonight from [time] to [time] is a Double Vote Hour, presented by [Sponsor].
> Packs whose checkout starts in that hour count twice.

Text: only to people who asked for updates.

### 4. The Open Round's last day

The deadline is real, so you can say so.

Text:
> Last day! The Open Round closes tonight at 8. As of [time], [Pet] is [n]
> votes [above / below] the bracket line. [link]

Facebook:
> The Open Round closes tonight at 8 p.m. As of [time], [Pet] is [n] votes
> [above the line / from the top 16]. If you've been meaning to vote, today's
> the day: payments have to go through by 8. [link]

Story: a countdown sticker to 8 p.m.

### 5. [Pet] made the bracket

Everybody starts over, so say that first.

Text:
> [Pet] made the Sweet 16 as the No. [seed] seed! Votes reset to zero, and
> [Pet] plays [opponent] until [day] at 8 p.m.: [link]

Facebook:
> [Pet] made the bracket as the No. [seed] seed. Thank you, all of you! Every
> matchup starts at zero, so [Pet] needs votes all over again: [Pet] vs.
> [opponent] in the Sweet 16, closing [day] at 8 p.m. [link]

Story: the Story image, which now says [Pet] made the bracket.

[Opponent] belongs to a neighbor too. Root for [Pet], and leave the other pet
out of it.

### 6. The last day of a matchup

Text:
> [Pet]'s matchup closes tonight at 8. As of [time], [Pet] is [n] votes
> [ahead / behind]. [link]

Facebook:
> [Pet] vs. [opponent] closes tonight at 8 p.m. As of [time], [Pet] is [n]
> votes [ahead / behind]. A tie goes to the better seed. [link]

### 7. [Pet]'s run ends

Thank people. They'll remember it next year.

Facebook:
> [Pet]'s run ended in the [round]. Thank you to everyone who voted and shared.
> So far the contest has raised [raised] for Moments Foster Care. Good luck to
> [opponent]!

The figure is in the email WPR sends when [Pet] is out. Story: the Story image,
which now says "What a run, [Pet]!"

### 8. [Pet] is the champion

Facebook:

## Facebook groups

Local swap groups and community groups are some of the best places to tell
people about [Pet]. They're also somebody's living room, so:

- Read the group's rules first. Many swap groups allow promotion only on one
  day of the week or in one thread. If you can't tell, message an admin and
  ask.
- Post once per round in a group, at most, and don't bump your own post.
- Use your share link, so the preview shows [Pet]'s photo.
- Say plainly what it is: a Wausau Pilot & Review contest where votes come in
  packs, with 50% going to Moments Foster Care. Don't call it a donation or
  say it's tax-deductible.
- Pick groups where a pet post fits: your town's community group, a swap
  group's promotion day, a local pet group. Stay out of lost-and-found pet
  groups, rehoming groups and pet-loss groups.
- Don't comment "vote for mine" under other people's posts, including other
  entrants'.
- Be kind about every other pet. Each one belongs to somebody in the county.
- Don't run a giveaway, a raffle or a "vote and win" to get votes. Raffles and
  giveaways have legal rules of their own, and the contest can't vouch for
  yours.
- If an admin takes your post down, let it go.
- When someone asks a question you can't answer, point them to the rules
  ([rules link]) or to pets@wausaupilotandreview.com.

Don't tag Wausau Pilot & Review hoping for a share. WPR doesn't reshare
entrants' posts, which keeps owners' names off WPR's accounts.

## Asking family (yes, Grandma) to vote

Ask once per round at most, and make "no" easy to say. Say what it costs and
where the money goes. Offer ways to help that cost nothing: sharing the post,
forwarding it to a friend, coming over to meet [Pet].

A $5 pack is plenty. Nobody needs the big ones.

If Grandma wants to vote and hates typing card numbers into a phone, sit with
her and show her Apple Pay, or hand her the flyer and let the QR code do the
work. Don't take her card number over the phone to do it for her.

Vote packs aren't tax-deductible, so don't let anyone think they are. Someone
who would rather give to the animals directly can go to Moments Foster Care:
[Moments giving page].

On the phone or over coffee:

> You know [Pet] is in the Wausau Pilot & Review cutest pet contest? No
> pressure at all. If you want to vote, it's $5 for five votes, and 50% goes to
> Moments Foster Care, [a few words on what Moments does, in its own terms].
> If not, I'd love it if you showed your friends the picture.

By text:

> Hi Grandma! [Pet] is in a contest in the Wausau Pilot & Review. If you'd like
> to vote, here's the link: [link]. Votes come in packs, starting at $5, and
> 50% goes to Moments Foster Care. Totally optional. Sharing it with your
> friends helps too.

Then say thank you, and don't send reminders to anyone who said no.

## Please don't

- Use someone else's card, or take their card number to buy votes for them.
- Promise anything in return for votes.
- Post the names of other entrants' owners, or guess who owns a pet.
- Post screenshots of anyone's receipts or emails.
- Promise anything about the champion beyond the title.

## If something goes wrong

Votes you bought aren't showing? They appear when the payment is confirmed,
usually within seconds. If a few minutes pass, email
pets@wausaupilotandreview.com with the time and the email address on your
receipt. Don't buy again to test it.

A payment made after a round's deadline is refunded automatically.

Someone is rude about [Pet] or posts your name on a Wausau Pilot & Review
post? Tell pets@wausaupilotandreview.com. WPR removes abuse from its own
posts.

# Social: captions, Reels, Stories, Facebook groups

Copy for WPR's own accounts. Entrants' copy is in `owners-toolkit.md`.
Bracketed words are placeholders: fill them from the Social desk or the
contest page at the moment of posting, never from memory.

## Rules for every post

- The first line has to work on its own. Facebook cuts captions at about 80
  characters, Instagram at about 125.
- One ask per post.
- Every post made while voting is open says vote totals reset every round.
- Any count that can still change goes out with "as of [time]." Refresh the
  Social desk right before downloading. Never post a "sample numbers" image.
- Pets and towns, never owners. Don't reshare an entrant's own post or Story:
  a reshare shows their account.
- Every matchup gets its card. WPR doesn't campaign for a pet.
- Vote packs are a purchase. Never describe buying one with "donate," "give,"
  "gift" or "tax-deductible." No prizes: the champion gets the title and
  bragging rights.
- Name a sponsor only with a label: "presented by [Sponsor]" in the copy and
  Meta's paid-partnership label on the post.
- Facebook: no hashtags; links are clickable, so the link ends the post.
  Instagram: 3 to 5 hashtags
  (`[contest hashtag] #Wausau #MarathonCounty #CentralWisconsin`), link in
  bio or on a Story link sticker.
- Tag WPR's own links so site analytics can tell channels apart. Put the tags
  before the `#`:
  `[contest page link]?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=pet-contest`
  (use `instagram` or `facebook-groups` as the source where it fits).
- Reply to comments within the first hour after posting. `moderation.md` has
  the lines for hard ones.

## Caption bank

Seven moments have hook variants, each built on a different mechanism (from
the hook-writer skill), then a full caption. Pick a hook, keep the body. The
supporting captions after them are shorter.

If the Social desk shows a Caption box under each card, start there for the
cards it draws. Its words come from the same numbers as the image. Check the
draft against the rules above before posting: add "as of [time]" to a live
count, the reset line if it's missing, and the sponsor label to anything that
names one. The bank covers what the desk can't write (entries week, Moments,
Double Vote Hours, the thank-you) and gives other hooks for every moment.

## Reels and TikTok scripts

Three production docs a reporter can shoot on a phone. Each works muted:
burn the on-screen text in, or use the platform's auto-captions and check
them. Audio is the reporter's own voice, with a bed from Meta's Sound
Collection if wanted; no songs from a personal library on a business account.
Export each video clean from the phone. Don't post the same file to both
apps with the other app's watermark on it.

Before posting any of the three:

- Every number came off the Social desk the same morning, and none came from
  a "sample numbers" preview.
- No owner's name anywhere, on screen or spoken. An owner appears on camera
  only with their consent, unnamed.
- No house numbers, street signs or license plates in a shot.
- Every count that can still change says "as of [time]."

### Reel 1: the bracket reveal

Goal: sends, and taps through to the bracket. Length 30 to 35 seconds. Posted
D15, late morning. Cover: the No. 1 seed's photo with "The Sweet 16 is set"
in the center of the frame, where the grid crop keeps it.

TikTok: open on the single best pet photo with "16 left" on screen in the first

## Facebook Groups playbook

Local swap groups and community groups are huge in Marathon County. They
belong to their members and admins, so WPR goes in as a guest.

### Don't start a group this year

A WPR-run contest group would need moderating evenings and weekends for three
weeks, then would sit empty for eleven months. Use the Page and the groups
that already exist. Revisit next year, and only if someone can tend a group
year-round.

### Build the list (D-13)

Keep a sheet with: group, town or area, admin, the promotion rule, allowed
days or threads, the date asked, and the answer.

Good fits: town community groups, buy-sell-swap groups with a promotion day or
thread, local pet-owner groups, neighborhood groups. Leave out lost-and-found
pet groups, rehoming groups, pet-loss and grief groups, and Moments' own spaces
unless Moments invites WPR in.

### Ask the admin first

> Hi [name], I'm [name] at Wausau Pilot & Review. We're running our Cutest Pet
> in Marathon County contest from [date] to [date], and 30% of vote pack sales
> goes to Moments Foster Care. Would it be OK to share it in [group]? We'd post
> at most four times: when entries open, when voting opens, when the bracket
> is set and when there's a champion. If you have a promotion day or thread,
> we'll use it. No problem at all if the answer is no.

No answer means no.

### Four posts per group, at most

D1, D8, D15 and D23. Each one is news. Post as WPR's Page where the group
allows Pages. Otherwise follow WPR's social media policy: a staff member who
says they're with WPR. Write each group's post fresh and space them out.
Pasting one text into a dozen groups in ten minutes looks like spam to
Facebook and to admins.

D1, entries open:

> Wausau Pilot & Review's Cutest Pet in Marathon County contest is open for
> entries until [day] at [time]. It's free: one phone photo, and an editor
> checks every entry. Voting starts [date], and 30% of vote pack sales after
> refunds goes to Moments Foster Care. [link]

D8, voting opens:

> Voting is open in the Cutest Pet in Marathon County contest. [N] pets are
> in, including pets from [town] and [town]. The top 16 make the bracket on
> [day], and vote totals reset every round. [link]

D15, the bracket:

> The Sweet 16 is set in the Cutest Pet in Marathon County contest. Every
> matchup starts at zero, and the round closes [day] at 8 p.m. [link]

D23, the champion:

> [Pet] of [town] is this year's Cutest Pet in Marathon County, and readers
> raised [shelter total] for Moments Foster Care. Thanks to [group]'s admins
> for letting us share. [link]

Thank a group's admins by name only with their OK.

### While a post is up

- Stay for the first hour to answer questions. `moderation.md` has the lines.
- If an admin removes a post, don't repost it. Thank them and cross the group
  off.
- Never push a particular pet in a group, and don't comment on entrants'
  posts asking for votes. Owners campaign; WPR reports.
- Point owners who ask about posting in groups to `owners-toolkit.md`.

Measure group posts by clicks (`utm_source=facebook-groups`) and by how many
posts admins remove, which should be none.

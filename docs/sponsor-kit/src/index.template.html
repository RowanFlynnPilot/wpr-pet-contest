Where the money goes

Vote packs are not donations and are not tax-deductible. [shelter share %] of vote pack sales, after refunds, goes to Moments Foster Care. Sponsorship fees are separate: they support WPR’s nonprofit newsroom and don’t change the shelter’s share.
